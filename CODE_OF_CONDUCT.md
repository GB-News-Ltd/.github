# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GB News engineering a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Examples of positive behaviour**

- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the audience, newsroom, and codebase
- Showing empathy toward other contributors
- Crediting others' work

**Unacceptable behaviour**

- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery in professional spaces
- Personal or political attacks unrelated to technical critique

## Enforcement

Report issues to **engineering-conduct@gbnews.uk** or your manager / HR partner. Leadership will review and may take action including warnings, temporary bans from project spaces, or permanent removal of access.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces (GitHub, Slack/Teams engineering channels, meetings) and when an individual is representing GB News engineering publicly.
